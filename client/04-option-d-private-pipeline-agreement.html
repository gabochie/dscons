Standard terms

1. Parties and purpose

This agreement is between DS Construction Limited ("DS", "the Client") and Gideon B. Abochie / Gabochie Marketing ("the Consultant"). Its purpose is to create a private, high-ticket business-development pipeline for DS's glazing and facade services without public advertising, public branded promotion, or ad-platform tracking.

2. Selected pathway

Tick one:

  • Pathway A — Done-For-You (managed). The Consultant builds the Heritage Portfolio, maps active projects and decision-makers, makes private introductions and secures qualified site-measurement invitations.
  • Pathway B — In-House Engine Build (consulting and training). The Consultant designs the scouting system, writes the scripts, builds the tracking tools, trains DS's nominated staff and co-pilots the first field loops; DS runs the system thereafter.

3. Definitions

  • Qualified invitation — a confirmed meeting or site visit with a named decision-maker (Project Manager, Site Engineer, Owner or Lead Architect) of an active project located in Greater Accra or the Ashanti Region; for a service DS actually provides; where the contact confirms they requested the quote or site inspection.
  • Introduced project — a project or decision-maker first contacted by the Consultant under this agreement and recorded in Annex B at the time of introduction.
  • Mobilisation deposit — the first payment DS receives from a client for an introduced project that confirms the award.
  • Contract value — the total value of the signed contract for an introduced project, excluding VAT and any later variation orders not part of the original award.

4. What the Consultant delivers

2 Decision-maker map: 30+ architectural/QS firms and 20+ active main contractors Yes Yes — framework and starter list
3 Weekly mapping of active, facade-ready construction sites Yes Co-piloted with DS staff
4 Private introductions and booked site-measurement invitations Yes — subject to the guarantee in §5 Training and QA — executed by DS staff

5. Monthly guarantee (Pathway A only)

The Consultant guarantees a minimum of three (3) qualified invitations per calendar month, with a working target of three to five (3–5). If fewer than three are verified in a month, the following month is at no charge (the monthly fee is credited), and the shortfall carries forward until met. The guarantee applies only while DS supplies the project assets and estimator availability described in §8.

6. Fees and payment

Item Pathway A — Done-For-You Pathway B — In-House Engine Build
Setup fee GHS 4,000 — one-time, payable at kickoff GHS 12,000–15,000 — 50% at kickoff, 50% at end of Month 2
Monthly fee GHS 5,500 / month — invoiced after the month's guarantee is verified GHS 3,000 / month — oversight and audit from Month 3
Success fee 1% of contract value on each introduced project that signs and pays a mobilisation deposit None — DS keeps 100% of the upside
Advertising spend GHS 0 — no ad spend is required or permitted under this agreement GHS 0

Payment terms: invoices are payable within seven (7) days. The Consultant issues no invoice for a Path A month in which the guarantee is not met; the work continues against the same invoice until the guarantee is satisfied.

7. Success-fee mechanics (Pathway A only)

  1. The Consultant records each introduction in Annex B and shares the entry with DS within two working days.
  2. DS signs or initials the entry to confirm the introduction and its date.
  3. When an introduced project signs a contract and pays its mobilisation deposit, the success fee becomes payable.
  4. The success fee is 1% of the contract value, invoiced within 14 days of the deposit being received, and payable within 7 days of invoice.
  5. Attribution: the introducing party is the one recorded first in Annex B. If DS can show a pre-existing written relationship with that decision-maker before the recorded introduction date, the success fee does not apply — the entry is marked "pre-existing" at signature.

8. What DS provides

  • High-resolution photographs and project details for the Heritage Portfolio (three iconic projects to start; more as available).
  • A named estimator who can attend site measurements and issue quotes.
  • Written approval of the Heritage Portfolio before any use.
  • Access to management for a short monthly review.
  • Prompt feedback on every introduction (attended / not attended / reason).

9. Confidentiality and tax-privacy commitments

The Consultant will not, in DS's name or with DS's branding:

  • run public Facebook, Instagram, Google or other sponsored advertising;
  • boost posts or publish public lead-generation forms;
  • install ad pixels, conversion tags, retargeting audiences or lookalike audiences;
  • upload DS customer lists or phone numbers to any advertising platform.

All outreach under this agreement is private and one-to-one (WhatsApp, email, phone or in person). The Consultant may describe DS's capabilities to a specific decision-maker, but will not publish DS's name, logo, contact details or project photographs publicly. Progress is reported privately to DS management.

10. Ownership and handover

On payment of the relevant fees, all deliverables created under this agreement — the Heritage Portfolio, target list, scripts and tracking database — belong to DS. The Consultant retains no lock-in over DS's accounts, data or client relationships.

11. Term, review and termination

  • Initial term: 60 days from kickoff.
  • Thereafter: month-to-month.
  • Termination: either party may end the agreement with 30 days' written notice. Fees for work already delivered and verified remain payable; the success fee survives termination for any introduction recorded in Annex B during the term.
  • Monthly review: the parties review the guarantee tracker (Annex A) and the introduced-project register (Annex B).

12. No guaranteed contract wins

The Consultant guarantees qualified introductions, not contract awards. Pricing, technical acceptance and contract terms remain entirely DS's decision. The Consultant makes no representation that any introduction will result in a signed contract.

13. Independence

The Consultant has no ownership, agency or commission relationship with any competing glazing fabricator, contractor or materials supplier. The Consultant's only interest under this agreement is DS's success.

14. Data protection

Personal data of decision-makers is collected only from lawful, private business sources; stored securely; used only for this engagement; and deleted or transferred to DS on termination.

15. Governing law and disputes

This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Ghana. The parties will first attempt good-faith resolution; if unresolved within 21 days, the matter may proceed to mediation or the courts of Ghana.

16. Signature

This is a commercial term sheet. Either party may request legal review before signature.
