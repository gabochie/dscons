Annex · Production Inputs

What we need from DS.

The book is built from DS’s own record. These are the inputs that turn the sample into the final portfolio.

Three iconic projects to start — ideally one institutional, one corporate and one luxury residential.
10–15 high-resolution photographs per project — exterior hero, detail, and process or site.
Project facts: name, location, sector, client, year, scope, systems and outcome.
Written permission to name any client or building where required.
Confirmed warranty terms and technical standards.
Factory and fleet photographs — one or two of each.
Founder or MD photograph on site, plus a short signed statement.
Company logo files in print quality — vector preferred.
Handling Protocol
Send only to a named decision-maker — Project Manager, Site Engineer, Owner or Lead Architect — of an active project.
Send by private WhatsApp or email, with a short personal message — never as a mass broadcast.
Record the send and recipient in the introduced-project register.
Never post the PDF, screenshots or project photographs on any public page, advert or social account.
Update the portfolio after every significant completed project — it is a living credential, not a one-off brochure.
