1. The whole proposal in five sentences

DS has done fine work for 26 years — everyone on a project site knows it. But when a new buyer looks for a glazing company today, they find your competitors first, not you. The praise from your past clients stays private instead of convincing the next buyer. And enquiries that arrive by phone or WhatsApp sometimes quietly die because nobody follows them up. We put a simple system around your business so nothing gets lost — and you only pay the larger share of our fee after you have seen the results yourself.

4. "What if we just wait?" — the honest answer

You can wait, and you keep the audit summary we gave you either way — nothing is owed. But two things are worth knowing:

  1. The season does not wait. Facade packages are decided before the dry-season build window. The firms that introduce themselves now are the ones written into the bills of quantities later. Waiting is free in cedis, but it can cost you the season.
  2. Your competitors are not waiting. Firms like K3 are already courting your institutional clients with polished presentations. None of them, however, has your 26 years of proof — that advantage is only worth something if buyers can see it.

5. The questions we expect — answered plainly

"We are not computer people." That is exactly why this is designed the way it is. Every technical thing stays on our side. You will see one short page of numbers each week, in plain words: enquiries that came in, quotes that went out, jobs won or lost and why. One of your staff spends about 45 minutes a day on the routine — we train them, start to finish.

"Can't we just use our own staff?" Yes — and Door 2's second table is precisely that: we build the system, write the scripts and train your people properly, then they run it and you keep everything it earns.

"We don't want our business in anybody's public eye." Then Door 2 was built for you. No advertisements in your name, no tracking tools, no public campaigns, nothing posted publicly. Progress reports arrive privately to management. The public sees nothing; your pipeline fills quietly.

"Consultants collect money and disappear." Look again at the payment tables. In Door 3, more than half the fee is released only when you verify results. In Door 2, a month without the promised introductions is a month you do not pay. We designed this so that the money only follows the work.

"What would you need from us?" Four small things: one trusted staff member to train; permission to approach past clients for reviews (you approve everything before it goes public); about 45 minutes of the MD's time on execution days; and access to your website and social accounts.

6. How to decide today — answer three questions

  1. Do you want new big jobs this coming building season? No → keep the audit summary with our compliments; no hard feelings. Yes → continue to question 2.
  2. Do you prefer to grow quietly or publicly? Quietly → Door 2, the Quiet Partnership. Publicly → Door 3, the Full Visibility System.
  3. Should we do the work, or should your own people run it? We do it → Door 2, first table ("We do it for you"). Our people → Door 2, second table ("We train your own people").

And whatever you choose — even "let me think about it" — the first step is the same and costs nothing: send the three photographs, and judge the free Heritage Book on its own merit.
