# Urgent Sales-System Design — DS Construction Limited (MVSS)

**Companion to:** `03-solutions-action-plan-internal.md` · `04-budget-and-consultant-fees-confidential.md`
**Prepared:** 6 November 2026
**Context:** Family-owned (MD + wife handling HR), 11–50 staff, quote-driven fabrication. Diagnosis: sales results leak from **absence of system** — not absence of product or reputation. This document defines what to install **immediately** (while the sustainable business system is built in parallel over 12–24 months).

---

## 1. Why sales is the emitting problem (logic chain)

| Observed issue | Root cause | System fix |
|---|---|---|
| Leads arrive by phone/WhatsApp randomly, from different numbers | No single intake point, no lead log | One WhatsApp Business line + CRM log (C1) |
| Quoting is ad-hoc → slow, inconsistent pricing | No rate card, no templates, no SLA | Price architecture + quoting SOP (C2) |
| Nothing is followed up → silent losses | No cadence ownership | 3-touch follow-up rule (C3) |
| Owner personally sells → no capacity, no visibility | No pipeline, no stage rules | Staged pipeline + weekly review (C4) |
| Family firm: nobody's clearly in charge of sales ops | Roles undefined | Role charter (Section 5) — important in a husband/wife structure |
| Cash gets strangled mid-project | Payment milestone not systematised | Deposit/milestone commercial standard (C5) |

## 2. The Minimum Viable Sales System (install in 30 days)

### C1 — Central intake & lead log (Week 1)
- **One public sales number/WhatsApp line** (the +233 20 811 7328 or +233 244 360 846) — print on website, GBP, social bios, vans, emails. Remove/retire secondary numbers for lead purposes; keep them as internal.
- **HubSpot Free CRM (or Zoho)**: every lead logged same day with: source (WhatsApp/call/site/walk-in/referral), segment, product interest, need-by date, next action + date.
- WhatsApp Business greeting flow: *auto-reply — welcome, ask 3 questions:* project type (home/office/institution), product category, timeframe → then route to the right playbook.

### C2 — Quote the fastest person in the room wins (Week 1–2)
Your biggest marketing asset is **quoting speed.** Ghanaian standard is weeks; set and publish:
- **Enquiry response SLA: within 24 hours** (message acknowledging + booking survey or sending rate card)
- **Standard products:** a **rate card** of pre-priced configs (sliding/spancasement windows, doors, burglarproofs, mosquito nets, showers) with size bands and material tiers — quote in **24–48h without a survey**.
- **Custom/jobs needing survey:** measure on-site, quote in **≤3 working days**, using BOQ template (Excel) with standard margin rules per product family (curtain walls vs cladding vs windows have different margin/risk profiles — decide them once, not per-quote).
- Pre-write the **contract template** + **survey form** + **customer-facing acceptance form**.

### C3 — Follow-up cadence (deploy in Week 1)
Rule of three: every quote gets **≥3 touches** — day 2 (answer questions), day 7 (call), day 14 (WhatsApp final nudge with a photo/proof asset tied to their product — "this is the exact system we installed for a school in Accra"). Log each touch. Quotes not followed = quotes lost by default.

### C4 — Pipeline & reviews (Week 2–4)
Stages: **New → Contacted → Survey booked → Quoted → Verbal → Won / Lost (record reason: price/competitor/schedule/no budget/silent)**.
- **Daily:** 15-min stand-up (MD + whoever is Sales Coordinator) — review all new leads & overdue follow-ups.
- **Weekly:** 30-min pipeline review — count quotes sent, value, wins, loss reasons, response SLA compliance → 5-line dashboard (see KPIs below).
- One screen: everything in CRM "Dashboard" — no fancy BI needed yet.

### C5 — Commercial standard (Week 1 decision, protect cash)
Ghana fabrication reality: client-upfront-funding needed for materials.
- Policy: **50–60% deposit** (materials) / **milestone payments** (fabrication 25–30%, installation 10–20%) / final on handover.
- Foundation for warranty marketing: published warranty terms are a sales asset; they require this policy to stay honest.

## 3. Three targeted playbooks (same pipeline, different scripts)

| Segment | Who leads | Playbook |
|---|---|---|
| **Institutional/Corporate B2B** (schools, developers, corporates, government) | MD personally | Request tender/spec docs → send case studies + trade pack (S5) → site visit done by MD → deliver quote in person, followed up with BOQ + warranty. One architect relationship can be worth 5 years of residential work. |
| **Architects & specifiers** | MD (relationship) | Spec library, WhatsApp check-ins on upcoming projects, invite to factory, "come design with us" tours. Spec-buyers don't buy on price — remove friction to *getting specified*. |
| **Residential homeowners** | Sales Coordinator (script-based) | WhatsApp-first: rate card + 3 project photos + deposit terms; survey where geographically sensible in Accra. Convert fast, low-touch; don't consume MD's time. |

## 4. KPI dashboard (from Week 2; review weekly)

| KPI | Standard/Target |
|---|---|
| responds to new leads → time-to-response | < 24 hours (target 4 h) |
| Time survey-to-quote | ≤ 3 working days |
| Quotes sent / month | 15–30 (from ~untracked today) |
| Win rate (quotes → won) | ≥ 30–40% fabrication norm |
| Average deal size + channel mix | tracked from month 1 (baseline) |
| Referrals & repeat share of won deals | ≥ 25–30% (should be high given 26-yr reputation now) |
| Lost-reason tally | monthly top-2 reasons reported to MD |

The Weekly 5-line dashboard gives you the numbers a full **rev ops system** will need later — so the MVSS isn't throwaway, it's the seed of the sustainable system.

## 5. Roles in a family firm (spell it out — prevent the classic conflict)

| Role | Person | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Sales Lead / closing senior deals | **MD (husband)** | B2B closing, pricing approval above pre-set bands, institutional relationships |
| Sales Coordinator (operations) | **To designate Week 1** | CRM hygiene, quote assembly, follow-up reminders, site-survey booking, KPI dashboard |
| HR & bookkeeping | **Founder-wife** (stays) | Continue HR; optionally own "customer service/complaints liaison" at handover stage if she prefers — do **not** put selling on her plate. In a family firm, separating family admin from sales operations avoids resentment and overload. |
| Production interface | Workshop foreman | Survey scheduling, fabrication scheduling priority board fed by won quotes |

**Design principle:** one person has the CRM owner title. It doesn't need to be the wife; it can be a trusted admin or first sales hire later — but it must be **one named person on the org chart**, not "everyone." When your Year-1 marketing budget (Tier 2) funds an added **BD executive**, he/she inherits pipeline stage-3 (surveying/qualifying) from the MD.

## 6. Path from MVSS → sustainable business system

- **Now (Days 0–30):** MVSS live—intake, SLA, templates, cadence, dashboard, roles.
- **Months 2–6:** Harden via handbooks — quoting SOP v2, commission policy for salespeople, objection library, CRM automation (auto follow-up reminders).
- **Months 6–12:** Integrate with marketing engine (source-level ROI: which channels create quotes/wins); extend to project-ops interface (survey → fabrication → install → warranty statuses visible to sales for status-update calls — a huge trust point in Ghana).
- **Year 2 (with the 5-year roadmap):** Rev-ops system — CRM + accounting + production planning connected, forecasting by product line, partner-developer portal; consider **ISO 9001-style documented process** for institutional tendering edge.

**Quick-win by design:** this system can be live in 30 days at near-GHS 0 cash cost (free CRM, WhatsApp, templates, discipline). Its returns are immediate — faster quotes and consistent follow-up are worth more than any ad spend at this stage of the business.
