# 08 — Value at Stake: the revenue leak model (internal)

**Purpose:** quantify, with stated assumptions, what DS Construction is leaving on the table in its current condition — and show exactly where the engagement KPIs plug into the money math.
**Status:** indicative pending calibration with DS's real sales data (kickoff discovery = audit §8 questions). Every figure carries its assumption.

---

## 1. The funnel and the three leaks

A fabrication business converts: **Visibility → Enquiries → Quotes → Won jobs → Repeat/Referrals.**

| # | Leak | Mechanism observed | Industry norm effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | **Visibility leak** | No Google reviews anywhere, no managed GBP, product pages not search-visible, socials dormant → not in the shortlist for buyers who start online (increasingly all of them) | Enquiry volume suppressed vs.Indexed, reviewed competitors |
| L2 | **Follow-up leak** | Quotes/rest handle by memory; no cadence, no log | Across AEC/construction, **40–60% of issued quotes are never followed up** and quietly die (cite: standard pipeline-management benchmark; will present as industry norm, not DS-measured) |
| L3 | **Speed/trust leak** | No rate card → quotes take days-weeks; no public warranty/proof → buyers default to competitor with paperwork & reviews | Slower quotes lose deals at equal price; documented proof supports price defence |

## 3. Leak quantification (annualised, mid-case)

| Leak | Math | Annual value lost (mid) | Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| L2 Follow-up | 25 quotes/mo × 40–60% un-followed → recoverable wins ≈ quote volume × un-followed share × recover rate 12% ≈ **1.4 jobs/mo** × GHS 130k | **≈ GHS 2.2M/yr** | GHS 1.0–3.4M |
| L1 Visibility | Search/review-led enquiry uplift realistic for a first mover in a niche with *zero* entrenched review power: +25–75% on non-referral enquiry flow (mid assumptions: 10 leads/mo today → +3–7 qualified/mo × 25% win × GHS 130k... hedged: +1–1.8 jobs/mo) | **≈ GHS 1.8–2.8M/yr** | GHS 0.9–5.0M |
| L3 Speed & proof | Faster (24–48h) quotes + rate card + warranty/proof → improve quote→win by 3–8 points (22%→25–30%) on ~300 quotes/yr → +0.9–2.4 jobs/mo × GHS 130k | **≈ GHS 1.4–3.7M/yr** | GHS 0.7–5.6M |
| **Total plausible leak** | | **≈ GHS 5.4–7.2M/yr mid** | **≈ GHS 2.6–14M/yr** |

**Working number for conversation: GHS 5M+/year left on the table, most plausibly — vs. engagement max cost GHS 105k (≈ 2% of the leak).** Even at the most cautious corner of the range, one recovered mid-size institutional job repays the whole engagement.

**Honesty rules when presenting:**
- Present as a *model with levers*, not a promise: "each assumption gets calibrated with your real numbers in Week 1 — then the model becomes our revenue plan, not a sales pitch."
- Never name a competitor's revenue or invent DS internals; all figures carry "to be calibrated" until real data arrives.
- The headline sanity check: even a 10% improvement effect on the funnel (from today's baseline) ≈ GHS 0.9M+/yr at mid-case — enough to justify the entire fee 8× over.

## 4. Where the KPIs come in — KPI-to-money mapping

The Day-90 KPI sheet is not admin — it's the control panel of this model. Each KPI repairs a leak, and the payment tranches gate on them so the consultant only earns when the funnel mechanics actually change:

| KPI (Day-90 sheet) | Repairs leak | Model variable it moves | Path to revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25+ verified reviews + GBP top-3 ambition | L1 | Enquiry volume (non-referral) | More quotes entering the pipeline |
| 10 search-friendly pages live & indexed | L1 | Enquiry volume per product line | Same, per product segment |
| Response <24h + logged leads (CRM) | L2 + L3 | Contact rate, follow-up compliance | Fewer enquiries lost before quoting |
| Rate card + ≤3-day custom quotes | L3 | Quote speed → win rate | Same list, better hit rate |
| Weekly dashboard + outreach log (100 targets) | L2/L3 | Pipeline discipline + B2B enquiry flow | Institutional repeat channel |
| Results bonus (≥3 attributable won jobs) | Outcome | Actual wins + revenue-by-source | Direct, auditable |

**Two layers of KPI, deliberately:**
- **Week 0–90 (process/output KPIs — the Day-90 sheet):** things the consultant directly controls — pages, reviews, response time, cadence, dashboard. These gate payment tranches (Part 2/Part 3). They prove *the system exists and runs*.
- **Month 4 onward (outcome KPIs — reported monthly, bonus-gated):** leads by source, quotes, win rate, attributable won jobs/revenue. These prove *the money* — and because L1–L3 fixes need market time to compound, they gate only the optional bonus + retainer, never the build phase. That's the honest sequencing a client should expect.
