## B. What You Should Charge as Main Consultant

### Tools with fee card here: 3 options to present to DS

**Option 1 — Project-based engagement (strategy only)**
- Scope: audit + competitive intelligence + strategy + 90-day plan ("what you already produced, formalised")
- Fee: **GHS 40,000–90,000 flat** (~US$2,700–6,000) depending on seniority positioning — or US$ scope if presented remotely internationally
- Timeline: 3–5 weeks · Payment: 50% kickoff / 50% delivery

**Option 2 — Strategy + 6-month implementation oversight (Recommended)**
- Scope: Option 1 + retainership to steer execution (weekly catch-ups, SOPs, QA of content/ads, dashboard reviews)
- Fees: **GHS 45,000–60,000 project fee + GHS 12,000–20,000/month retainer** (6 months ≈ GHS 117–180k total)
- Payment: 40/30/30 at kickoff / month 3 / month 6
- Rationale: the retainer protects the client from "shelf document" syndrome — the single biggest failure mode of audits (~GHS 13–33k/mo affordable within Tier 2 people line)

**Option 3 — Performance-linked model (blend)**
- Reduced base retainer (e.g., GHS 10,000/mo) + **success bonus: 5–7.5% of revenue value of signed contracts from new marketing channels** (capped, e.g., ≤GHS 100k) OR 15% of ad-spend management if you run media directly
- Only use if DS will share sales data transparently; otherwise skip

### Pricing anchors for the negotiation

- **Value anchor:** implementing Tier 2 properly plausibly adds GHS 500k–2M annual revenue (2× quote flow at institutional price points). A GHS 150–250k total Year-1 consultant package is **<20% of expected incremental revenue** — lead with this, not hours.
- **Market comparables:** multinational agencies in Accra charge US$8–15k/month retainers (NTH – 41K brand levels GHS 8–25k/mo for SME social management alone). A sole senior consultant at GHS 15–20k/mo + project fee sits mid-market — fair given your unique strategy+AI efficiency.
- **Do not quote on days/hours alone** for the audit (it invites squeezing); present deliverables and outcomes instead.
- **Walk-in terms:** retainer assumes client provides access (analytics, client list, brand assets); three-month initial term, 30-day notice; expenses (GIC travel, ad budget) billed separately at cost.

### Suggested pitch (one line)

> "Fixed project fee of GHS 50,000 for the full audit and 5-year strategy; GHS 15,000/month for six months to steer implementation through the 90-day sprint — with milestone reviews tied to the KPI dashboard we've built."

## UPDATE (6 Nov 2026, afternoon) — Client-trust restructure adopted

Client asked whether payment should be tied to KPIs/results (fear: "consultants take money, no results"). Adopted structure in `client/01` §5:

**Option B (recommended): GHS 40k foundation + GHS 25k delivery tranche (day-60 acceptance) + GHS 25k results tranche (day-90 KPI sheet) + GHS 5k/mo oversight (months 4–6, KPI-gated) + optional GHS 15k results bonus (≥3 attributable won contracts by month 6, or 2.5% of attributable value, capped).**

Why not 100% pay-on-results (say this to the client honestly): pure contingency forces corner-cutting and destroys quality leverage on both sides; the foundation fee covers genuinely keepable assets (audit, templates, systems build, training). ~57% of the fee is behind the KPI gate.

**Protection notes for Gideon:**
- Day-90 KPI sheet = signed at kickoff; every KPI verifiable on sight (screenshots/URLs/logs). No subjective clauses.
- KPI-gated monthly retainer: invoice issued only if client confirms monthly KPIs; otherwise work continues next month against the same invoice — never double-charge for a failed month.
- Foundation fee covers ~Weeks 1–6 of real labor + assets; if terminated at month 4, walk-away math is neutral-to-positive.
- Results-source attribution rule (for the bonus): first-touch channel logged in CRM decides; otherwise no bonus claim — keeps it auditable and dispute-proof.

## UPDATE (7 Nov 2026) — Option D added: The Private Growth Partnership

Client-facing `client/01` now carries a fourth, recommended option drawn from the `leadgenstrat` angle: a 100% private, legacy-led B2B pipeline (Heritage Portfolio → active-site safari → decision-maker introductions). No public branded ads, no sponsored posts, no pixel/audience uploads.

**Two pathways (client chooses):**
- **Path A — Done-For-You:** GHS 4,000 setup + GHS 5,500/month + 1% success fee on introduced contracts that sign and pay a mobilisation deposit. Written guarantee: 3–5 qualified site-measurement invitations/month, or the next month is free.
- **Path B — In-House Engine Build:** GHS 12,000–15,000 setup/training (Months 1–2) + GHS 3,000/month oversight from Month 3, no success fee (DS keeps 100% upside with its own team). This answers the director's "we'll just use our own staff" instinct.

**Why these numbers work for Gideon:**
- Path A first-year base = GHS 70,000 + commission — lower entry than Option B, but the 1% commission is the real upside on GHS 500k–1M+ facade contracts (GHS 5,000–10,000+ per closed project).
- Path B captures the in-house demand at a high one-time fee and leaves a low-effort oversight retainer, with no commission leakage.
- "Qualified invitation" is defined tightly in the agreement (named decision-maker, active project, target region, service DS provides, contact confirms request) so the guarantee cannot be gamed.
- The free sample Heritage Portfolio (3 project photos) is the proof-first close — build it before asking for money, per the `leadgenstrat` chat.

**Placement rule:** lead with Option D when the client's stated fear is revenue-authority visibility or when they want to internalise the work. Keep Options A–C as the digital-visibility alternatives; they can be layered later, but no paid ads before Day 90 and only with written consent.
