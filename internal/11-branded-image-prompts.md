## Tips for best results

- Generate at 16:9 or 4:3 aspect ratio.
- Add "no watermark, no text except the logo" to keep typography clean.
- If the logo renders incorrectly, regenerate with the logo image attached and the phrase: "use the attached logo exactly, do not redraw or reinterpret it."
- For consistency across the five images, keep the brand palette (charcoal, gold #dca62c, navy coveralls) and Ghanaian setting in every prompt.
