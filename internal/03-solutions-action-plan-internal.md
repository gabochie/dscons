## A. Strategy Reset (Month 0 — the 2 decisions that change everything)

### A0-1. Pick a defensible position
Stop competing as "a leading glazing specialist." Own a position competitors can't copy quickly:

> **"Ghana's most experienced aluminium glazing factory — trusted by Ghana's leading institutions for 25+ years, manufacturing everything in-house at our own Accra plant."**

Three pillars to say, show, and prove repeatedly:
1. **Factory-backed** (in-house manufacture = quality control, custom work, faster delivery)
2. **Institution-trusted** (Ashesi, University of Ghana, Toyota Ghana, GNAT, GEA — logos with real project photos)
3. **Nationwide + Togo** (own fleet = reach and reliability)

### A0-2. Make WhatsApp the sales channel spine
Ghana's buyer journey runs on WhatsApp. Every website page, social post, email, and van decal routes to one WhatsApp Business line with a greeting-and-qualify flow.

## D. 6–12 Month Horizon (Year 1 completion)

1. **Developer partnership programme** — formal relationships with 5 estate/residential developers (Glenshire-style): a "Windows-as-Standard" partner offer for new builds; case-study pipeline.
2. **Energy-efficiency content advantage** — given glazing is a big driver of Ghana's building cost inflation, publish a "Smart Glazing Investment Guide" (double-glazing vs. AC costs, 5-yr total cost). Position DS as the people who *lower your lifecycle cost*, not the cheapest quote.
3. **Speculator/trainee umbrella** — short technical workshops for architects (1 per quarter, hosted at DS factory) = latent sales channel + goodwill + content.
4. **Video series & press** — 1 flagship documentary-grade piece ("25 years of the buildings you walk past") pitched to business/Ghana architecture media; strengthens B2B gravitas.
5. **Measurement maturity** — quarterly dashboard: leads by source, quote→win rate, revenue per channel, review velocity. Kill or scale channels by data, not habit.
6. If Dr. marketing function stabilises: hire/contract a dedicated marketing coordinator (part-time acceptable) — sustainability requires ownership; audits without an owner decay into shelf documents.

## F. Risks & Guardrails

- **Capacity risk:** demand generation without quote capacity hurts delivery. Stage paid ads behind a proven <24h quote SLA.
- **Price-war temptation:** don't compete on price with informal fabricators; compete on proof, warranty, and speed-to-quote.
- **Single-point dependency:** if marketing runs through one person, document SOPs (posting calendar, review ask script, CRM hygiene) so it survives holidays/departure.
- **Data honesty:** all figures herein are desk-researched; validate with client internals (Section 8 of audit) before major spend.
